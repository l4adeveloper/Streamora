# Streamora study poster

Historical asset, superseded by the code-native SVG in
`src/components/study-banner.tsx`. The current home page does not use generated
images. The former WebP is archived at
`.preview/streamora-study-poster-archived.webp` (1672 × 941; 168 KB).

Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. The generated PNG was encoded as
WebP for delivery; the composition and visual content were preserved.

The poster depicts collaborative study in a campus library. It contains no
workspace interface, device screens, baked-in titles, logos, or UI elements.
All headings, branding, and authentication controls are rendered separately by
the website for accessibility and responsive layout.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-viewport photographic poster
background for Streamora, a Vietnamese community learning and live collaboration
product. Create a premium editorial photograph, landscape 16:9. Three Vietnamese
university students in their early twenties studying together at a light oak
table in a spacious contemporary campus library, candid focused interaction,
one pointing at an open notebook, another listening, books and pencils, natural
human expressions, believable hands and understated everyday clothing. Large
windows, soft late-afternoon daylight, blue-gray architectural tones with warm
wood and a little greenery. Composition: students and table occupy the LEFT
half and lower-left of the frame; the RIGHT 45 percent is quiet softly
out-of-focus architecture with generous negative space because a white login
form will overlay it. Keep the upper left relatively calm for a short headline
added separately in HTML. Sophisticated realistic lighting, natural skin and
paper texture, gentle photographic depth of field, restrained authentic color
grading, no glossy stock-photo posing. This is a photograph, not a UI mockup.
Absolutely no screens, no computers, no phones, no app interfaces, no floating
cards, no icons, no diagrams, no gradients-as-art, no rendered 3D objects, no
letters, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Opaque background.
